About This Tool

This AI-powered Resume Optimizer helps you match your resume to job descriptions by identifying aligned skills and suggesting improvements. It is designed to support students, job seekers, and professionals who want to tailor their resume for better alignment with job opportunities. All processing is done locally and temporarily to protect your privacy.

Confidentiality

This tool does not store or share any uploaded resumes or job descriptions. All processing is done in your local session, and the data is cleared immediately after analysis. For best results, avoid uploading personal identifiers like your full address or phone number.
